I'm a founding software engineer at Electio Technologies, where I build backend systems and infrastructure from the ground up.

Before Electio, I worked at Mark43 on mission-critical software for public safety agencies, and earlier at Palantir. I keep gravitating toward technology for critical infrastructure and public institutions.

Before that, I was a Fellow at the Federal Trade Commission and co-authored a study on privacy vulnerabilities in darknet markets, which was covered by The Washington Post and The Sunday Times. I studied computer science at Harvard, with a minor in government.
